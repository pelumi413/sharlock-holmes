Our Campaigns

Driving Change
on Every Road

Five focused interventions targeting the leading causes of road crashes in Nigeria — from human behaviour to physical road conditions.

01
Seatbelt Safety

Buckle Up

Nigeria's seatbelt compliance rate remains dangerously low — particularly among rear-seat passengers and in commercial vehicles. The Buckle Up campaign deploys field advocates at major road junctions, motor parks, and markets to demonstrate correct seatbelt use and communicate the life-saving statistics.

We partner with state traffic management agencies to conduct compliance checks, distribute free seatbelt extenders to larger users, and train vehicle operators on passenger safety briefings before departure.

02
Pedestrian Safety

Cross Safely

Pedestrians account for a significant share of road fatalities in Nigeria, with many occurring at unmarked crossings or in the absence of functional pedestrian infrastructure. Cross Safely targets both pedestrians and drivers — teaching pedestrians to use designated crossings and motorists to yield without exception.

School-focused sub-programmes bring the campaign directly to pupils through interactive assemblies, crossing simulations, and student road safety clubs.

03
Speed Management

Slow Down

Excessive speed is the single largest contributing factor in fatal crashes on Nigerian roads. Slow Down works at community level to establish speed norms through signage, speed cushion advocacy, and community speed monitoring programmes where residents record and report speeding vehicles.

We engage local government areas to introduce enforceable 30km/h zones around schools and markets, supported by public education on the physics of speed and stopping distance.

04
Lane Discipline

Overtake Wisely

Dangerous overtaking and poor lane discipline are leading causes of head-on collisions on inter-state highways. Overtake Wisely uses dramatic impact narratives — survivor testimonies and crash reconstruction videos — distributed through social media and radio to viscerally demonstrate the consequences of reckless overtaking.

Practical training sessions at driving schools reinforce the correct procedure: signal, mirror, blind spot check, clear gap assessment, and clean return to lane.

Partner With Us
05
Visibility

Be Seen Be Safe

Cyclists, motorcyclists, and pedestrians walking after dark face extreme risk due to inadequate lighting and reflective wear. Be Seen Be Safe distributes thousands of retroreflective vests, arm bands, and bicycle lights to vulnerable road users in low-income neighbourhoods.

Alongside hardware distribution, the campaign runs mobile screenings of visibility demonstration films and engages okada and tricycle operators — who move millions of Nigerians daily — on safe lighting practices.
