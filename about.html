Safer roads, by design.

We're a Nigerian non-profit working to make every journey home safer — through road safety education, public-awareness campaigns, and community-led advocacy.

Our story

Born from preventable loss.
Now in 9 LGAs.

RAP Initiative began in response to a road safety crisis that touches almost every Nigerian household. With tens of thousands of road deaths each year — most of them entirely preventable — we set out to put road safety at the heart of community life.

What started as a single school workshop has grown into a multi-program organisation working with students, drivers, transport unions, local government, and the Federal Road Safety Corps. Our method hasn't changed: listen first, train alongside, and stay long enough for the change to hold.

Mission, vision & values

Why we do what we do

Mission

To save lives by championing road safety across Nigeria — through education, advocacy, and community engagement that empowers individuals to make life-saving choices.

Vision

A Nigeria where every road journey ends safely at home — where road safety is shared culture, not just enforcement.

Values

Lives first. Evidence-based campaigns. Long commitments over quick wins. Community knowledge over outsider blueprints. Dignity in every interaction.
