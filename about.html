Who We Are

About RAP Initiative

A non-governmental organisation committed to eliminating preventable road deaths and injuries across Nigeria through targeted campaigns, research, and community action.

Our Mission

Saving Lives.
One Road
at a Time.

The Road Accident Prevention (RAP) Initiative was founded on a simple but urgent conviction: that the majority of road crashes are preventable. We exist to prove it — through relentless public education, community mobilisation, and policy advocacy.

Operating across Nigeria's urban and rural corridors, we combine grassroots outreach with evidence-based campaigns to change the behaviours and conditions that cause crashes.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Safety First

Every decision, campaign, and partnership is evaluated through the lens of its impact on road safety outcomes.

Evidence-Based

We ground our campaigns in data and research, measuring impact rigorously and adapting when the evidence demands it.

Community-Led

Sustainable change starts within communities. We build local champions and equip them to lead safety transformations.

Transparency

We publish our impact data, finances, and methodologies openly — trust is the foundation of our credibility.
