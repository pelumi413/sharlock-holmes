Our Campaigns

Driving Change
on Every Road

Five targeted campaigns addressing the leading causes of road crashes in Nigeria, from seatbelt compliance to pedestrian safety.

01
Seatbelt Safety

Buckle Up

Promoting consistent seatbelt use for all vehicle occupants on every journey, no matter how short.

02
Pedestrian Safety

Cross Safely

Educating pedestrians and drivers on proper use of zebra crossings and safe crossing practices.

03
Speed Management

Slow Down

Advocating for speed limits adherence in residential zones, school areas, and high-risk corridors.

04
Lane Discipline

Overtake Wisely

Teaching safe overtaking techniques and lane discipline to prevent head-on collisions.

05
Visibility

Be Seen Be Safe

Equipping vulnerable road users — cyclists and pedestrians — with visibility tools for nighttime safety.

Public Awareness

Visualising
Safety.

Through compelling multimedia content, live events, and community outreach, we make road safety tangible — translating data into stories that move people to act.
