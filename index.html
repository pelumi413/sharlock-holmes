What we do

Three pillars, one shared mission

RAP Initiative is built on a simple promise — that every person deserves the tools, the platform, and the power to shape their own future.

Education for all

Scholarships, learning hubs, and digital literacy programs that keep girls and boys in school — and build skills for the next decade.

Learn more →

Empowerment & livelihoods

Vocational training, micro-grants, and mentorship that move women and youth from dependency to independence — and into local leadership.

Learn more →

Advocacy & voice

Campaigns, research, and community organising that move policy makers and bring lived experience into the rooms where decisions get made.

Our story

Driven by community.
Designed for change.

Since 2017, RAP Initiative has worked with grassroots leaders to design programs that actually fit the communities they serve. We don't parachute in solutions — we listen, co-create, and stay long enough to see them through.

  • ✓
    Community-first design

    Every initiative starts with a listening tour and ends with local ownership.

  • ✓
    Measurable, transparent impact

    We publish what we measure — and adjust when the data tells us to.

  • ✓
    Long-term partnerships

    We commit to a community for at least three years. Trust isn't a quarterly metric.

Programs

Where the work happens

Four flagship programs, designed and run with — never just for — the communities they serve.

Flagship

RAP Scholars

Full-tuition scholarships, mentorship, and career-readiness coaching for girls in secondary and tertiary education.

Livelihoods

She Earns

Vocational training and seed grants for women starting micro-businesses.

Advocacy

Voices Forward

Youth-led campaigns and policy briefings that turn lived experience into systemic change.

Health

Healthy Beginnings

Maternal & adolescent health outreach: screenings, supplies, education, and peer support in 9 LGAs.
