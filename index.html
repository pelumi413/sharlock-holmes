Our story

Born from preventable loss.
Built to save lives.

Every year, tens of thousands of Nigerian families lose someone on roads that could have been safer. RAP Initiative exists so fewer families have to make that call.

We work alongside schools, drivers, transport unions, government partners, and grassroots community leaders to put road safety at the heart of public life — through training that respects local context, campaigns that travel, and advocacy that's grounded in real data.

  • ✓
    Community-first design

    Every program starts with the people who use the road — drivers, parents, students, pedestrians.

  • ✓
    Evidence-based campaigns

    We measure what works, publish what we learn, and adjust when the data tells us to.

  • ✓
    Long-term partnerships

    Road culture changes slowly. We stay for the duration — not just a campaign cycle.

Programs

Where the work happens

Four flagship programs — each designed with the communities, drivers, and institutions they serve.

Flagship

Safer Schools

Interactive road safety curricula and workshops for primary and secondary schools — pedestrian safety, helmet & seatbelt use, and what to do at the roadside.

Driver training

Safer Drivers

Community workshops with taxi, bus, freight, and motorcycle riders — co-designed with transport unions and motor parks.

Advocacy

Voices on the Road

Public awareness campaigns and policy briefings to local government, FRSC, and state lawmakers — turning lived experience into safer regulation.

Community

Eyes on the Road

Citizen-led black-spot mapping and community road audits that feed real data to authorities and inform our next campaign.
