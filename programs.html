Education

RAP Scholars

Full-tuition scholarships, mentorship, and career-readiness coaching for girls in secondary and tertiary education. We pair every scholar with a mentor — and stay in touch long after graduation.

  • ✓
    3,200+ scholarships

    since 2017

  • ✓

    vs. 64% regional average

Livelihoods

She Earns

A 12-week vocational training program followed by a seed grant and a year of business coaching. Cohorts cover tailoring, agriprocessing, beauty services, and digital freelancing — chosen by the women themselves.

  • ✓
    1,400 women trained

    across 9 cohorts

  • ✓
    78% still in business

    two years after the program

  • ✓
    Average income +210%

    over pre-program baseline
